# Code of Conduct
We follow the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
Be respectful, welcome newcomers, accept constructive criticism, and focus on what is best for the community. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks and publishing others' private information are not tolerated. Maintainers may remove content and ban participants who violate this. Report issues privately to the maintainers (contact to be added at publication).
